# anything the service imports at runtime has to be here — requirements-train.txt
# is for training only and is never installed on the box.

# until 2026-08-19, which is why LOAD_BERT_MODEL=false was the only workable
# setting in production regardless of whether weights were present.
#

# ~2GB of nvidia libraries that would never be used.
